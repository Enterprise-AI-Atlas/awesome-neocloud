# AGENTS.md — Awesome Neocloud

## Purpose

This repository is a curated registry of resources for researching the Neocloud ecosystem, AI-native cloud providers, GPU-as-a-Service, and the NVIDIA Cloud Partner landscape. It is intended for both human researchers and automated agents.

## Scope

Cover resources that help evaluate, compare, or understand Neocloud providers:

- Neocloud and GPU-as-a-Service providers.
- NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP) and Reference Platform documentation.
- Independent benchmarks, ratings, and market research.
- Financial, funding, IPO, and sustainability resources relevant to Neoclouds.
- Software for building or operating AI-native cloud infrastructure.

## Style

- Follow the same format as the other `awesome-*` repositories in the Enterprise AI Atlas organization.
- Use badges: `Official`, `Provider`, `Research`, `Analysis`, `Rating`, `Platform`.
- Prefer official documentation and authoritative sources.
- Keep descriptions factual and concise.

## Maintenance

- Run `./scripts/validate-links.sh` before finishing any edit.
- Place entries under the research topic they primarily serve.
- Update or remove stale entries during periodic reviews.

## Important reminders

- Do not provide legal or investment advice.
- Verify that links resolve and are current.
- Maintain the disclaimer that this is an independent, unendorsed curation.
